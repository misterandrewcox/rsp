Red Stain Press — approved website branding assets

These files implement the initial brand masters approved by Andrew Cox and effective 2026-10-04. They do not create a new brand identity or guideline revision.

Controlling visual usage guidance:
https://redstainpress.sharepoint.com/Shared%20Documents/Publishing%20Operations/12%20Brand%20Website%20and%20Platform%20Operations/Branding/Initial%20Masters/RSP-Brand-Guidelines-v0.2.pdf

The approved kit RSP-Brand-Kit-v0.2 supplies the primary horizontal vector, symbol, and browser/application icons. The vector logo symbol remains solid #9B0000; its single-line Red Stain Press wordmark uses outlined Red Hat Display Bold, with Press at the same size, weight, and color as the other words.

Smudge-Newsletter files are unchanged PNG exports from the approved RSP-Smudge-Brand-Kit-v0.1 nested in the company kit. They retain the mascot's transparent background and original illustration shading.

Fonts are full-character variable WOFF2 packages of authentic Red Hat Display upright and italic fonts. See assets/fonts/FONT-SOURCE.txt and the accompanying SIL Open Font Licenses.

asset-manifest.json records exact source files, SHA-256 hashes and any packaging steps. Approved logo and mascot artwork has not been redrawn. Existing legacy assets elsewhere in the website are outside this asset integration and have not been removed.
