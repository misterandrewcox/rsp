Website webfont packaging — 2026-10-04
The upright font is packaged losslessly as RedHatDisplay-Variable.woff2 from the approved kit TTF, with its full 300–900 weight range and all 406 mapped characters retained.
The authentic italic variable font is from the official Red Hat font project:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RedHatOfficial/RedHatFont/master/fonts/Proportional/RedHatDisplay/variable/RedHatDisplay-Italic-VF.ttf
It is packaged losslessly as RedHatDisplay-Italic-Variable.woff2, with its full 300–900 weight range and all 405 mapped characters retained. No synthetic slant or font subsetting is used.
The italic upstream license is provided in OFL-RedHatOfficial.txt.
