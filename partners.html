The publishing approach

A clear focus.
Room for discovery.

Red Stain Press is a gay-owned independent publisher developing a boys’ love graphic novel list, with plans for original comics and licensed titles in print and digital editions.

Red Stain is getting to know creators and exploring potential titles, with attention to how each edition could reach readers.

Red Stain draws on experience in strategy, analytics, and business development at Penguin Random House and Macmillan, alongside a long-standing love of BL.
