Red Stain Press is taking shape as an imprint with a clear editorial identity: graphic novels about men falling for men.

Our planned list brings together original work and licensed comics, with print and digital editions. We’re developing relationships with creators and laying the foundations for a publishing operation that can support a focused catalog.

The founder’s background spans more than a decade across two Big Five publishers. That industry experience sits alongside a close connection to the genre and its readers.
