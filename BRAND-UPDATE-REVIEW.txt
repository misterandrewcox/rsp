Implemented
- All four full pages and the legacy submissions redirect use the new browser icons.
- All eight header/footer identities use the supplied outlined horizontal SVG.
  The full company name is one line, with every word at equal size and weight.
- Standalone corporate marks use the exact supplied splash in solid #9B0000.
  Red marks on dark sections have a calm paper surface to retain visibility.
- Red Hat Display is hosted locally, with authentic upright and italic variable fonts.
  Headings/labels/buttons use Bold; body copy uses Regular.
- Paper remains #F8F7F3. Gold is #F0D28D. Ink is #121212.
  Dark-section text accents use gold rather than low-contrast red text.
- Shared horizontal gutters replace conflicting hero/header/section padding.
  The hero's separate maximum-width cap is removed, including the wide-screen
  alignment mismatch. Intentional section-label/body columns remain.
- Header navigation collapses before the larger one-line lockup becomes cramped.
  Company-name spans prevent static copy splitting the name over two lines.
- Smudge supports the Home and Our Story newsletter areas, using the approved
  transparent Newsletter pose on paper. The full silhouette is retained.
- Social link previews use an evenly scaled approved lockup on brand paper.
- Approved prose is retained apart from replacing short company references with
  the full name. Founder/business experience, selective submission contact,
  artwork, services, destinations, consent, and fields remain as approved.
- CSS and changed form-script references have updated content cache versions.
