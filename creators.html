Before you submit

A few essentials.

These guidelines help us understand the work and whether it can be considered for our list.

Original work and adult characters

Original work only, without another creator’s characters or setting. Creators must be 18 or older, and all lead characters must be adults.

Portfolios are welcome

You can submit an illustration portfolio without a complete graphic novel. Sequential art samples are helpful when you have them.

Existing rights agreements

If your work is a WEBTOON Original, existing agreements may prevent us from working with it. Let us know about any publishing or platform commitments.

No polished pitch required

A clear introduction, your project’s stage, and a link to the work are a good start. Tell us what you think we should know.
