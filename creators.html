Before you submit

A few essentials.

Please check these before sending your work.

Original work and adult leads

Submit original work, rather than work using another creator’s characters or setting. You must be 18 or older, and all lead characters must be adults.

Portfolios are welcome

You don’t need a complete comic to share an illustration portfolio. Include sequential art samples if you have them.

Publishing and platform agreements

Tell us about any existing publishing or platform agreements. These may affect whether we can work with your comic, including if it is a WEBTOON Original.

No polished pitch required

Introduce yourself, tell us what you’re sharing and how far along it is, and send a link. A clear introduction is enough to get started.
