Why Red Stain Press

BL is the whole point.

Slow-burn office romances. Childhood friends realizing they’re definitely not just friends. Terrible decisions made by attractive men.

These are the stories we love. We wanted a publishing home built around them: a focused imprint with its own list, real relationships with creators, and a submission process that doesn’t assume you already know how publishing works.

Red Stain Press is building its first catalog of original graphic novels and licensed comics, with plans for print and digital editions. We welcome creators at different stages, from their first comic to an established body of work.

“The imprint you’d trust
with your favorite trope.”
The experience behind it

Industry experience.
Personal investment in the stories.

Our founder has more than a decade of publishing experience across two Big Five publishers.

That background informs how we’re building Red Stain Press: a distinctive editorial direction, thoughtful relationships with creators, and careful planning for each graphic novel, from the first pages to the finished edition.

We’re still getting the presses ready. Right now, we’re developing our first list and getting to know the artists and writers who may help define it.
