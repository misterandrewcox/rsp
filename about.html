About Us

A small press with a very specific type

Red Stain Press publishes graphic novels about men falling for men. BL, yaoi, gay romance — whatever you call it, we think it deserves a publishing home built by people who actually love the genre.

We started Red Stain Press because the stories we loved most — slow-burn office romances, childhood friends realizing they’re definitely not just friends, terrible decisions made by attractive men — kept being treated like a niche instead of something worth building around. We wanted an imprint where BL isn’t the side list. It’s the whole point.

Red Stain Press was founded by a publishing industry veteran with more than a decade of experience across two Big Five publishers — and, just as importantly, by someone who has been reading this genre for a very long time. We’re building the kind of imprint we wanted to see: one with its own catalog, real relationships with creators, and a submission process that doesn’t assume you already know how publishing works.

We're not trying to be everything. We're trying to be the imprint you'd trust with your favorite trope.

What we publish

Original graphic novels and licensed comics, in print and digitally. We work with creators at every stage — from people making their first book to studios with an established audience. If it’s a story about men falling for men, we’re interested.

  • Slow burn
  • Slice of life
  • Fantasy & historical
  • Office & adult romance
  • Campus stories
  • Found family
  • Old man yaoi
  • Omegaverse

Where we are right now
