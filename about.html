Why Red Stain

BL is the whole point.

Slow-burn office romances. Childhood friends realizing they’re definitely not just friends. Terrible decisions made by attractive men.

We love these stories enough to build a publisher around them. Red Stain Press is a gay-owned independent publisher devoted to boys’ love graphic novels, with plans for original comics and licensed titles in print and digital editions.

We’re starting with the stories and the people who make them. Finished comics, promising works in progress, and illustration portfolios are welcome. You don’t need publishing connections to introduce yourself.

Why I’m building
Red Stain.

As a gay man, I’ve often struggled to find stories about gay relationships that made me feel like I belonged.

BL manga, manhwa, and webcomics changed that. For the first time, I saw myself clearly in the stories I was reading. I felt welcomed, understood, and part of the world on the page.

That feeling is why I founded Red Stain Press.

We’re a gay-owned independent publisher devoted to boys’ love. Our commitment is to these stories, the artists who create them, and the readers who find something of themselves in their pages.

At Red Stain, BL is the whole point. It will always be our focus.

I’m building the kind of publisher I wanted to find as a reader: one that gives these stories its full attention and supports the people who make them possible.

The experience behind it

A reader’s heart.
A head for publishing.

Red Stain Press was founded by Andrew Cox, a BL reader with a background in strategy, analytics, and the business of publishing at Penguin Random House and Macmillan.

His work has ranged from understanding how books sell across global markets to supporting publisher growth, financial planning, and acquisitions. That experience informs the planning behind Red Stain: how we build the business, evaluate opportunities, and bring our publishing ambitions to life.

Now, he’s putting boys’ love at the center of the work. He’s building an independent publisher with plans for original comics and licensed titles, in print and digital editions.

The taste is personal. The planning is serious.
