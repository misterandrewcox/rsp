Submissions

Bring us your story

We're building our first catalog and want to hear from writers and artists working in boys' love, in any stage of a project. Here's how it works.

What we're looking for

Original boys' love fiction — complete manuscripts, works in progress, or portfolios from artists interested in illustration or sequential art work. We're open to most sub-genres and tones.

  • Original work only — no fan fiction or content using another creator's characters or setting
  • Creators must be 18 or older to submit
  • All lead characters in submitted work must be adults
  • Manuscripts in English, or with an available English translation
  • Artists: a portfolio link is fine in place of a full manuscript

How submitting works

  1. Prepare your materials

    A one-page synopsis, the first three chapters or 20–30 pages, and a short note about yourself. Artists can send a portfolio link instead.

  2. Send it our way

    Fill out the form below, or email us directly if you'd rather send files as attachments.

  3. We read it

    Every submission is read by our editorial team. Because we're a small press getting off the ground, response times will vary — we'll always let you know we've received it.

  4. We follow up

    If it's a fit, we'll reach out to talk next steps. If it's not right for us, we'll try to tell you why.
